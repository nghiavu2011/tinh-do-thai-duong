## 3. High-Risk / Cautionary Topic Guidelines

- **Feather Coverage:** Adult *T. rex* skin impressions show scales in specific body patches, while close basal relatives (*Dilong*, *Yutyrannus*) had filamentous integument. Do NOT claim adult *T. rex* was entirely feathered or entirely featherless as an undisputed fact.
- **Locomotion / Speed:** Estimates range from walking speeds of 10–12 km/h to running calculations of 20–29 km/h. Do NOT post single top-speed statistics in early hero cards.
- **Dietary Behavior:** Clear adaptations for bone crushing and predation exist alongside opportunistic scavenging potential. Avoid false binary dichotomies ("pure apex hunter" vs "pure scavenger").
